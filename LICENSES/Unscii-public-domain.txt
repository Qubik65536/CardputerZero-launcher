Unscii bitmap fonts
Author: Viznut (http://viznut.fi/unscii/)
Status: released by the author into the public domain.

The 8x8 glyph tables in templates/*/src/font.* and scripts/make_app_icon.py
were extracted from LVGL's converted copy of unscii-8
(SDK/github_source/lvgl/lvgl_9_5/lvgl/src/font/lv_font_unscii_8.c, generated
with "lv_font_conv --bpp 1 --size 8 --font unscii-8.ttf -r 0x20-0x7F"),
which LVGL redistributes within its MIT-licensed source tree
(LICENSES/LVGL-MIT.txt). Only the printable ASCII range 0x20-0x7F is used.
